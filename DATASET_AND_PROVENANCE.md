# CU-BEMS Dataset Documentation & Data Provenance

## 1. Overview of the CU-BEMS Dataset
The **CU-BEMS** (Chulalongkorn University Building Energy Management System) dataset is a public benchmark for smart commercial building energy and Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) research.

- **Source**: Miller et al., Kaggle & Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok, Thailand.
- **Building Type**: Multi-story academic and research building (Engineering Faculty).
- **Climate Zone**: Tropical Savanna / Monsoon (Köppen *Aw* / *Am*), characterized by high temperatures year-round (26°C – 36°C) and elevated relative humidity (60% – 95%).
- **Temporal Span**: January 1, 2018 through December 31, 2019 (2 full operational years).
- **Dataset Structure**: 14 individual CSV files (Floors 1 through 7 for 2018 and 2019).
- **Sampling Frequency**: Recorded raw at 1-minute intervals, resampled to 1-hour uniform steps for reinforcement learning and thermal modeling.

## 2. Sensor Outage Realities & Missing Data Policy

A critical scientific discovery made during exploratory data analysis is that **sensor coverage is highly heterogeneous and subject to substantial hardware outages**.

### Sensor Availability Findings
1. **HVAC Power Meters**: Maintained nearly 100% operational uptime throughout 2018 and 2019 across all floors and zones.
2. **Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) Sensors (`degC`, `RH%`, `lux`)**:
   - Experienced prolonged multi-month outages due to transducer disconnections and network gateway failures.
   - In `2018Floor2.csv`:
     - `z1_S1(degC)`: Valid readings present from **July 5, 2018 to September 28, 2018** (1,767 valid hourly intervals after bounded resampling). Prior to July and after September, values are missing or offline.
     - `z4_S1(degC)`: Completely offline (0 valid temperature readings across the entire year 2018).
     - `z2_S1(degC)`: Valid readings present during July – September 2018, making Zone 2 the designated valid cross-zone benchmark domain.
   - In `2019Floor2.csv`:
     - `z1_S1(degC)`: Valid readings present from **February 1, 2019 to December 31, 2019** (7,980 valid hourly intervals).

### Bounded Missing Data Interpolation Policy
- **No Unrestricted `ffill()` or `bfill()`**: Forward-filling or backward-filling across multi-day outages is strictly prohibited as it fabricates constant temperatures.
- **Bounded Interpolation ($\le 3$ Hours)**: Only short telemetry dropouts ($\le 3$ hours) are linearly interpolated.
- **Outage Trimming**: Extended outages (> 3 hours) are dropped, and simulation episodes are constrained to continuous blocks.
- **Quality Tracking**: A binary quality mask (`temp_is_interpolated`, `rh_is_interpolated`) tracks every interpolated observation.

## 3. Data Provenance Registry & Scientific Honesty

In compliance with the project's scientific directives, every parameter, feature, and control variable is classified into one of seven rigorous provenance categories:

1. `OBSERVED`: Direct hardware sensor measurements in CU-BEMS telemetry.
2. `DERIVED`: Deterministic mathematical combinations of observed variables.
3. `EXTERNAL`: Validated external data sources with timestamps.
4. `FITTED`: Estimated using a documented parameter fitting procedure.
5. `FITTED_WITH_CAVEAT`: Estimated under physical or unobservability limitations.
6. `SIMULATED_ASSUMED`: Operational hypotheses assumed for simulation because historical observations were unrecorded.
7. `SIMULATED`: Dynamic variables generated by the simulation model during rollouts.

## 4. Benchmark Splitting Protocol (Zero Leakage)

1. **Source Domain Splitting**:
   - **Training Set (70%)**: $t \in [t_{start}, t_{70\%}]$ ($1,236$ hours).
   - **Testing Set (30%)**: $t \in [t_{70\%}, t_{end}]$ ($531$ hours).
   - Strict chronological ordering: $\max(t_{train}) < \min(t_{test})$.
2. **Target Domain 3-Way Partitioning**:
   - **Calibration Slice (20%)**: For equipment K-Means clustering and grey-box parameter fitting.
   - **Adaptation Slice (40%)**: For agent fine-tuning budget experiments.
   - **Held-Out Test Slice (40%)**: Evaluated only for reporting benchmark performance.
   - Zero overlap across all three slices.
